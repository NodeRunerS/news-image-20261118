## 1. LEAD — Ukraine smashes the Kerch Bridge (Europe / war)
- Ukraine struck the 19 km bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea for a
  second night in a row (Tue 17 Nov); the main road arch and a span of the
  road section collapsed into the Kerch Strait; road traffic suspended,
  rail damaged. Crimea's Russian-installed governor Sergei Aksyonov said
  2 people were killed. Zelenskyy called it an "unprecedented" operation
  by the SBU and all of Ukraine's forces, using drones, Neptune missiles
  and the new Flamingo missile. Russia said it intercepted 1,441 drones.
- Russia hit two Kyiv bridges in return; at least 2 people killed in Kyiv.
- Sources: BusinessWorld (Reuters-verified video), 18 Nov

## 2. US–Iran tanker war in the Gulf (Middle East)
- The US said it sank five Iranian oil tankers — Kaviz, Charminar,
  Horizon 1 and Riesco in the Gulf of Oman, Derya near Kharg Island —
  after Iran's Revolutionary Guards allegedly tried twice to target a
  US warship. Iran said it attacked ten ships near the Strait of Hormuz
  and fired missiles at US forces at the Al-Azraq base in Jordan, and
  declared a maritime restricted area from Chabahar into the Gulf of Oman
  and the Arabian Sea. US aircraft struck an IRGC missile launch site on
  Qeshm Island; an LPG tanker caught fire in Hormuz.
- Sources: Morning Headlines Roundup (18 Nov)

  Daily Baku, 18 Nov <https://dailybaku.az/en/category/dunya> ("US confirms

## 5. UN warns of cold deaths in Gaza (Middle East / humanitarian)
- The UN warned of more deaths from cold as winter sets in: 1.7 million
  people remain displaced in Gaza, about 1.4 million face acute hunger,
  nearly two million need shelter. Foreign ministers of Australia, Canada,
  Italy and Britain urged Israel to remove obstacles to aid deliveries.
- Sources: Al Jazeera / WFP / IOM via Morning Headlines Roundup, 18 Nov
  (same link as story 2).

## 6. COP31 climate talks on a knife-edge (Climate)
- COP31 in Antalya entered its decisive phase: the draft "cover decision"
  published by co-host Australia contains 144 disputed items, from fossil
  fuel phaseout to finance; EU envoy Wopke Hoekstra warned it could make
  the process "untenable". Small island states demand action and funding
  ($500m resilience, $200m early warning). Talks scheduled to end Fri 20 Nov;
  the US is absent for a second year.
- Sources: Dawn, 18 Nov <https://www.dawn.com/news/2045268/tensions-rise-over-proposed-deal-at-cop31> ;
  Reuters, 17–18 Nov <https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop31-off-rocky-start-2026-11-17/> ;
  Mathrubhumi, 18 Nov.
